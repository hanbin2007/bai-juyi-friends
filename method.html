How the names were tested 測試方法

The same AI model answered the same 35 names twice: once from the poem title alone, and once with historical sources. Every answer was then checked by hand against the annotated edition of Bai Juyi's works.

The two rounds 兩輪

Round A · title only

僅詩題

The AI sees one poem title and the name to identify, for example 元九 in 酬元九對新栽竹有懷見寄. It names the person and gives its confidence (high, medium or low), or declines.

Round B · title + sources

詩題＋史料

The AI answers the same names again, this time with historical sources to draw on, and lists the sources its answer relies on. These are shown in each entry's details.

Steps 步驟

  1. Choose the names35 forms of address of the type surname + sibling rank (姓＋行第) in 27 poem titles by Bai Juyi, each with its place in 箋校 (volume and page) noted beforehand.
  2. Round AAsk the AI to identify each name from the title alone and record the answer and stated confidence.
  3. Round BAsk again with sources, and record the answer, confidence and the sources the AI cited.
  4. Check by handLook up each entry in 《白居易集箋校》, with 《唐人行第録》 as a second reference, and note what the editor says.
  5. Give a verdictMark each answer in each round with one of the verdicts below. The site is rebuilt from the record sheet.

How answers were checked 核對

  • Main reference: Zhu Jincheng's 《白居易集箋校》 (Bai, 2020). Its notes (箋) name the person behind a form of address where the editor could establish it, or say 名未詳 (“name unknown”).
  • Second reference: Cen Zhongmian's 《唐人行第録》 (Cen, 1978), a dictionary of Tang people listed by surname and sibling rank.
  • Access: both books were read in the 汇典·典籍整理文献数据库 database, one entry at a time and by hand. Nothing was scraped or downloaded from it.
  • Quoting: the site quotes 箋校 only briefly, a name or a short phrase, always with volume and page (卷·頁).

Verdicts 判定

Limits 局限

  • Small sample. 35 names from 27 poems by one poet. The counts describe this set only and are not a measure of AI accuracy in general.
  • Memorised answers. Bai Juyi's best-known friends, such as 元九 (Yuan Zhen) and 劉二十八 (Liu Yuxi), are widely discussed online. A correct answer about a famous name may come from the AI having seen that scholarship, not from working it out.
  • One model, one run. Another model, or the same model on another day, may answer differently.
  • The reference is scholarship too. 箋校 leaves some names unidentified and sometimes disagrees with 唐人行第録. Verdicts follow 箋校.
  • Poem text. The poems shown in each entry come from an open digital edition of 全唐詩 that leaves out Bai Juyi's own notes (自注). They are there for reading and were not used for the verdicts.
