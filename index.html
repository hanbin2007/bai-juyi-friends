Identifying Bai Juyi's Friends with AI and Historical Sources

以 AI 與史料考白居易詩題中的友人

Tang poets often named friends by surname and sibling rank, or 行第: 元九, “Yuan the Ninth”, is Yuan Zhen. This site records whether an AI can identify 35 such names in Bai Juyi's poem titles, first from the title alone, then with sources, with every answer checked against Zhu Jincheng's 《白居易集箋校》.
